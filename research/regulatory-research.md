Do not implement future integration or assume current API termination based on media.

## Operational leads

- [Airport official access page](https://www.aeropuertomonteria.co/como-llegar) describes Metrosinú departures from Terminal around 07:00, hourly, service until 17:30. Page does not specify day types, exact last departure or return timetable; treat as provisional operator-facing evidence, not legal timetable.
- [Historical cooperation proposal](https://es.scribd.com/document/496690318/RecaudarDelSinu-V2) describes a general 05:00–22:00 demand window and route variants. Not current route-specific operating hours or authorization. Historical list includes Canta Claro–Villa Cielo and Vallejo as parts of itineraries; not proof of present independent routes.
- [2026 urban fare report](https://www.elheraldo.co/cordoba/2026/01/11/la-tarifa-del-transporte-urbano-colectivo-en-monteria-sera-de-3500/?openSidebar=true) reports proposed/agreed COP 3500 pending decree; [later report](https://monteriahoy.com/noticias/chn/analizan-nuevas-tarifas-de-transporte-publico-y-taxis-para-2026/) mentions COP 3300. Conflicting secondary evidence; no fare imported.
