## Original Decreto 0402 and CONPES 4178 review (2026-11-25)

User supplied the signed eight-page Decreto 0402, dated 2023-06-23 (p.8), and the approved 93-page CONPES 4178 dated 2025-12-23. The decree is now verified from the page images rather than a secondary transcription. Its recitals (p.2) explicitly say the 2010 ETLF was revised in 2019 and adjusted again following changed travel demand and COVID-19. Article 1, paragraph 2 (p.4) requires the implementation plan and operational design to form part of the separate service reorganization act. Paragraphs 3–4 (p.5) set a maximum 12-month route implementation period from operator habilitation, not the date of the decree, and require a reorganization resolution and operator habilitation (or public tender). Article 3 (p.6) confirms the four parties of Resolution 0586/2020 and centralized planned operation; this arrangement is subject to subsequent acts, including the reported loss of enforceability in Resolution 0394/2024. Article 4 (p.7, amended art.6 of Decreto 0544) explicitly requires a service reorganization resolution based on the revised operational study, plus an annex defining quality indicators; evaluate after first year. Article 5 assigns planning/control to ente gestor and operational scheduling to operator. Article 3 states collected information belongs to the territorial entity or its delegate, not that the public has unrestricted access. No route-specific tables or annex included.

CONPES 4178 pp.21–22 records traditional TPC still in operation in 2025, not the intended SETP; p.39 expressly references the updated ETLF and gradual operation with 60% of routes certified with operational SRC/SGCF to access full national funding. No updated route count or per-route schedule is specified for Montería. The 12 trunk/9 feeder routes on p.17 refer to original CONPES 3638 (2010), not the 2025 network. Table 28 p.77 provides COP 20,043,061,482 in 2027 for fleet procurement; table 18 pp.60–61 is expected benefits from original design, not current operations. Bibliography p.92 gives two new document leads: Ministerio de Transporte (2025), Plan de acción CONPES: SETP Montería, SITM MIO Cali y SITM Valle de Aburrá – Metroplús; and Ministerio de Transporte (2025c), Informe trimestral de ejecución del convenio de cofinanciación del Sistema Estratégico de Transporte Público de Montería (SETP), enero–marzo 2025. Seek these and latest quarterly reports. Primary target is the actual Resolución de reorganización del servicio with plan/diseño operacional and annexes; searches on 2026-11-25 found no original or verified number. Recent reports [2026-11-23](https://gsnoticias.com/tras-17-anos-de-espera-monteria-pone-en-marcha-su-nuevo-setp/) and [2026-11-24](https://larazon.co/monteria-estrenara-su-setp-en-enero-de-2027-la-flota-renovada-llegaria-en-septiembre/) describe agreement and expected January 2027 service transition, not legal confirmation of route changes.
